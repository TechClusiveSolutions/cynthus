# Cynthus App Notes

## General

The app should have five tabs: Planning, Tracking, Health, Search, and More.

## Planning Tab

This is where you can plan meals, Events, Exercise, and more. It is also where you will find recipes.

## Tracking Tab

This is where you can keep track of inventory, Past meals, past events, and more.

## Health Tab

This is where you will find current and past health statistics. You can also message your health services providers.

## Search Tab

This is where you can search for meals, recipes, exercise plans, events, health data, and more.

## More Tab

This is where you can configure the app, set preferences, update your profile, and more.
