The app is an AI-powered lifestyle companion app. It has four primary functions.

1. to assist individuals and groups with the planning and execution of day-to-day nutrition-related tasks
2. to assist small to medium sized businesses with the planning and execution of scheduled engagements involving food-related activities
3. to assist healthcare professionals with the collection and monitoring of client health data
4. to assist individuals, groups, businesses, and service providers in the tracking and management of nutrition-related inventory
