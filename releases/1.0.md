## Signal broker

Every service that uses the bus follows `SIGNAL_BROKER` (`servicebus` by default, `rabbitmq`, or `memory`) as of
shared v0.11.1. The dev stack on JMac ran on RabbitMQ 4.4 with the Service Bus emulator stopped on 2026-11-11
(core issue 183). The apps below still list their first tag; their merged work on `dev` is not yet tagged and
will get a version when the set is released.
